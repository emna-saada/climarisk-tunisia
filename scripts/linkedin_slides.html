The method

From climate data
to expected loss

1
Place the loans

2
Measure four hazards

Heat and drought from five CMIP6 models (NASA), 100-year floods (WRI Aqueduct), wildfire fuel (ESA WorldCover).

3
Weight by sector

A cereal farm fears drought, a beach hotel fears floods: one score per loan.

4
Translate into credit risk

The score raises the probability of default; floods raise the loss on land and property.

PD′ = PD × (1 + β × score)  ·  EL = PD′ × LGD′ × EAD

ClimaRisk Tunisia07 / 07
What I learned

Three lessons

1
The credit link matters most.

2
Indicators can disagree.

Two respected drought indicators rank regions in opposite order. The right one depends on what is financed.

3
Honesty over precision.

The NGFS Phase V damage function relies on a retracted paper, so I left it out. Physical risk only, synthetic data.
